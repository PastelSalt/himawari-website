**In short: this website gives visitors no cookies, loads nothing from other sites, and counts visits without knowing who you are. Himawari itself sends nothing anywhere.**

## This website

### What we count

When a page loads, your browser tells our server which page it was. We use that to know how many people visit and which pages they read. For each page view we keep:

- the page's address on this site, such as `/download`, and its language;
- the name of the site that linked you here, if any, such as `github.com` (not the full address);
- whether you're on a phone, tablet, or computer, and your browser's family, such as Firefox (not its version or your system's details);
- your country, as a two-letter code, worked out by our host from your connection;
- a visitor code, so that one person reading three pages counts as one visitor.

The visitor code is a scrambled mix of your connection's address, your browser's description, and a random value that changes every day. We delete each day's random value after two days, so codes from different days can't be matched, and a code can't be turned back into an address. **We never store your IP address**, your browser's full description, or anything that identifies you.

We don't count anything when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, and we don't count crawlers.

Single page views are deleted after 90 days. After that we keep only the daily totals for each page.

### What we don't do

- **No cookies for visitors.** The only cookie the site sets is the sign-in cookie of its admin panel, which Moeko Works uses to post news. Visitors never get it.
- **No trackers, ads, or analytics services.** Fonts, images, and scripts all come from this site. Nothing loads from anywhere else.
- **Your Motion choice stays in your browser.** The switch saves `on` or `off` in your browser's local storage (`hws:prefs`). It's never sent to us.

### Who handles the data

The site runs on [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) in Singapore, and the counts are stored in a [Neon](https://neon.com/privacy-policy) database, also in Singapore. Like any web host, Vercel handles your connection to serve the pages. The download button and many links lead to [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), which has its own privacy statement.

## Himawari, the software

**Himawari sends nothing anywhere.** Studio, the tools, and every game made with Himawari have no servers, accounts, telemetry, or analytics. Your stories, your art, and your players' saves stay on the computer they're on. A finished game makes no network calls.

The one exception is the optional AI help, and only when you use it. With Studio's built-in assistant, the text you send and the parts of your project it reads go directly from your PC to Anthropic, under your own API key. With Claude Code or the Claude desktop app, that app decides what is sent, under your own Claude account.

The full details are in [Himawari's privacy notes](https://github.com/PastelSalt/himawari-c/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
